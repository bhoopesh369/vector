What filtering can change

Low-pass filtering reduces high-frequency content. Stationary calibration subtracts a constant gyro bias after stillness checks. It does not restore clipped or missing readings, remove gravity, or establish drift-free orientation/position. Filtering does not shift sample timestamps; segment edges still have transient effects.
